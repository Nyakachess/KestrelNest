# Code of conduct

Nest exists so that people can make their first contributions in public without fear. Everyone here was new once.

## Expected

- Be kind in review. Point at the line, say what to change and why, and assume the author did their best with what they knew.
- Ask before assuming. A question costs less than a wrong guess.
- Keep feedback about the work, never about the person.
- Credit people for their work, in the changelog and in conversation.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, slurs or unwelcome attention, in any channel connected to this project.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Dismissing a contribution because of who made it.

## Reporting

Write to hello@kestrelcollective.org or to any maintainer listed in CODEOWNERS. Reports are read by at least two maintainers, kept confidential, and answered within a week. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments, and may remove someone from the project, when this code is broken.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues and pull requests, the site, and any Kestrel space where Nest is discussed.
